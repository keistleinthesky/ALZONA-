My humble hut
may look tiny,
but the veggies around
it, sure are many.
Yam beans and eggplants,
wing'd beans and peanuts,
string, hyacinth and lima beans.
Winter melon and loofah,
bottl' gourd, squash, et cetera.
There is more, amiga,
radish, mustard, yeah!
Onions, tomatoes
garlic and ginger.
If you look all around,
sesame seeds abound!
